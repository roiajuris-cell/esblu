## Which cookies and similar technologies Esblu uses

Esblu uses only one cookie of its own, which is strictly necessary/functional — to remember the selected interface language. Login and session persistence continue to be handled exclusively by the browser's **localStorage** technology, used by the Supabase Auth login library — not by cookies.

Esblu does not use any optional (analytics, marketing, or tracking) cookies. Since the only cookie used is strictly necessary/functional (not a tracking cookie, and not used for profiling or marketing), we do not display a cookie banner or consent request — this would serve no real purpose in this case.

## Overview of browser storage used

- **Name/key:** `sb-<project-ref>-auth-token`
- **Type:** localStorage (not a cookie)
- **Purpose:** Maintaining login (session token) between visits to the site
- **Provider:** Supabase Auth
- **Category:** Strictly necessary

- **Name/key:** `esblu_locale`
- **Type:** cookie
- **Purpose:** Remembering the selected interface language (Slovak/German/English) across pages and visits, including for visitors before registration or login
- **Provider:** Esblu (its own, no third party)
- **Duration:** approximately 1 year, or until manually changed or browser data is cleared
- **Category:** Strictly necessary/functional — not used for tracking, profiling, or marketing, only to remember the language preference

This storage is cleared upon logout (localStorage) or when browser data is manually cleared. It does not contain tracking identifiers for any purpose other than login and language preference.

## Analytics, advertising, tracking

Esblu does not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tool (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar) and does not integrate any external tracking script.

## Future changes

If Esblu were to start using additional cookies or similar technologies beyond those described above in the future (e.g., optional analytics), this page will be updated, and a consent tool (cookie banner) offering an equally easy way to accept or reject will be displayed before such technologies are deployed, as required by applicable rules.
